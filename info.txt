# sleep
Enable S3 Sleep State in BIOS (CRITICAL):
 -Before installing Arch, reboot into your ThinkPad BIOS (F1 at startup).
 - Go to Config > Power > Sleep State and set it to Linux (or S3). This stops the laptop battery from draining while the lid is closed.
